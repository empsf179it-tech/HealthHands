Frequently Asked Questions

How quickly can you arrange care?

We can typically arrange a care coordinator consultation within 24 hours, and often have a suitable caregiver ready to begin within 48 hours depending on the specific requirements and your location.

Do I have to sign a long-term contract?

No, we offer flexible care plans without lock-in contracts. You can adjust the level of support as your needs evolve, giving your family complete control over the process.

How do you match caregivers to clients?

Our proprietary matching system considers medical needs, personality traits, hobbies, and schedule availability. We believe a strong personal connection is just as important as clinical excellence.

Are your caregivers fully vetted?

Yes. Every single caregiver undergoes comprehensive national background checks, verification of clinical credentials, and multiple interviews before they are invited to join the HealthHands team.

How do you handle emergencies or sick coverage?

We provide 24/7 on-call support for all active clients. If your regular caregiver is sick or an emergency arises, our coordinator team will immediately arrange a highly qualified substitute to ensure completely continuous care.
